# Pixelcliq homepage direction

D2C-first growth agency with visible broader marketing capabilities. Keep portfolio empty until approved work is supplied.

## Research and decisions

- Digi Sidekick (https://www.digisidekick.com): leads with D2C positioning, performance/content/Shopify/social capabilities and prominent creative media. Adopt clear positioning and visual hierarchy, not its client/revenue claims or portfolio assets.
- Adymize (https://www.adymize.com): broader marketing services and funnels. Give lead generation, course/webinar funnels, social, websites, SEO and automation their own visible section rather than burying them in a sentence.
- Motion (https://motion.dev/docs/react-scroll-animations): use the already-installed library for a scroll-linked hero accent and process progress. No additional animation package or paid plugin needed.

## Implemented composition

1. Split editorial hero: immediate D2C positioning and CTA, cobalt creative/commerce/performance illustration. These are original service illustrations, not client projects or results.
2. Six illustrated capabilities, consistent card geometry and short deliverable labels.
3. Dedicated broader-services section with four prominent routes into an enquiry.
4. Five-stage interactive process: keyboard-accessible tabs, animated connection diagram, scroll-linked progress line and explicit deliverables.
5. Empty portfolio reservation, then FAQ and existing contact CTA/footer.

## Motion rules

Hero cards settle once; accent responds to scroll. No endless autoplay, scroll hijacking or cursor replacement. Process animates when a stage is selected. Reduced-motion preference removes entrance, path and scroll transforms. Main copy and CTAs never wait for animation.

## Remaining launch inputs

Approved portfolio, actual client proof and team imagery will add credibility beyond interface polish. Contact delivery integration and verified contact details remain launch dependencies; visual work does not configure a mailbox/CRM. No invented proof should substitute for those inputs.
