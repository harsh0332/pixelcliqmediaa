# Design cohesion update — 29 September

## Direction
Cobalt leads, navy provides contrast, cool white supports readable content, electric lime is a small emphasis colour. Removed the legacy olive/cream overrides that caused different sections to look unrelated.

## Implemented
- Home: full-width typographic statement above a creative exhibition, replacing the split copy/dashboard layout. Restored continuously rotating circular D2C stamp, reduced-motion fallback, clear enquiry and portfolio routes.
- Identity: compact pixel monogram with hover movement, stacked wordmark and matching browser icon.
- Services navigation: six distinct icon cards on a consistent cobalt/navy palette.
- Process: five distinct animated scenes for discovery, strategy, creative, launch and refinement; keyboard tabs, pause and reduced motion retained.
- Insights: six topic-specific editorial illustrations replace repeated photography and empty poster panels.
- Portfolio: dedicated `/creative-showcase`, prominently linked from home and work; collection copy no longer advertises a fixed film count.
- Shared pages: consistent text, border and inverse surface tokens across services, about, contact and portfolio.
